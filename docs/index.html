Safe gate: hold back answers a verifier doubts

A third model checks whether every claim in an answer is supported by the passages. The threshold is set with conformal risk control on half the questions and tested on the other half, so the expected rate of wrong answers shown stays under the target α. The guarantee is on average, not for every split; with ~52 calibration questions individual splits vary, which the 90th-percentile column shows.

numericHow many weeks of Pregnancy and Confinement Benefit does a team member receive in Hungary?

Baseline

Based on the provided context, the text does not explicitly state the number of weeks for "Pregnancy and Confinement Benefit" (CSED) as a standalone statutory duration. However, it states that if eligible, pay will be supplemented up to 100% pay (minus any CSED benefits received) for **up to 16 weeks**. Therefore, the team member receives pay supplementation for **up to 16 weeks**.

Final

In Hungary, a team member receives Pregnancy and Confinement Benefit (CSED) at a rate of 70% of their salary for 24 weeks [1].

Reference: 24 weeks

Read this before trusting the numbers

  • One corpus (94 handbook pages), one generator (qwen/qwen3.8-27b), one judge (openai/gpt-oss-120b). Results on your data will differ; that is the point of running the kit on it.
  • Correctness is graded by an LLM judge. Its verdicts were checked against blind grades from an independent LLM reviewer of a different model family from the generator and the judge (agreement κ = 0.93, n = 42). That is a check, not a human gold standard.
  • The multi-hop subset has only 14 questions; its intervals are wide.
  • Version-to-version differences inside overlapping intervals are not established improvements.
