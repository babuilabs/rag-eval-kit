What we changed

Small changes, each one measured.

We changed one thing at a time and kept only what helped.

01

Split documents by topic

Instead of every 1,000 characters. Wrong answers halved.

02

Tried better search

Keyword search on its own made things worse. We measured it, said so, and a re-ranker fixed it.

03

Taught it to cite, and to say “I don’t know”

The biggest single win. It stopped inventing answers.

04

Broke hard questions into simple searches

Comparison questions went from mostly wrong to mostly right.

Employee

If I move from GitLab LTD (UK) to GitLab France S.A.S., how will my annual leave entitlement change in terms of total days and the way it is tracked?

Before

Based on the provided context, it is not possible to determine how your annual leave entitlement will change in terms of total days or tracking methods when moving from GitLab LTD (UK) to GitLab France S.A.S.

Gave up
After

At GitLab LTD (UK), you are entitled to 28 days total (20 annual leave + 8 public holidays) which accrues from your start date [1]. At GitLab France S.A.S., you receive statutory vacation days per…

Correct
Full answers and the handbook’s answer

Before: Based on the provided context, it is not possible to determine how your annual leave entitlement will change in terms of total days or tracking methods when moving from GitLab LTD (UK) to GitLab France S.A.S. The context provided contains specific details for: 1. General/Unspecified Entitlement: 4 weeks minimum, no expiration, 14 days notice. 2. France (Statutory Sick Leave): 10 days after 6 months employment. 3. Australia (Long Service Leave & Family/Domestic Violence Leave): Specific state/territory rules and 10 days FDV leave. 4. Relocation Process: Instructions for resigning from France to move to a *new* country (not specifically detailing the UK-to-France transition or the resulting UK/France annual leave comparison). The text does not provide: * The specific annual leave entitlement for GitLab LTD (UK). * The specific annual leave entitlement for GitLab France S.A.S. (only sick leave is mentioned for France). * A comparison or transition rule for moving between these two specific entities. Therefore, the context does not contain the information required to answer the question.

After: At GitLab LTD (UK), you are entitled to 28 days total (20 annual leave + 8 public holidays) which accrues from your start date [1]. At GitLab France S.A.S., you receive statutory vacation days per French requirements plus additional RTT days, which are tracked separately from standard PTO [2]. In both entities, you must track your time in Workday, though France requires specific categorization for RTT [1][2].

Handbook: I would go from 28 days total (20 days annual leave + 8 public holidays) tracked in Workday as Paid Time Off to a French statutory vacation plus additional RTT days, also tracked in Workday but with separate RTT tracking.

The safety net in detailmethod and published targets

The checker is a third model family (neither the assistant nor the grader). Its threshold is set with conformal risk control on half the questions and tested on the other half, averaged over 1000 random splits. The guarantee holds on average, not on every split; with 52 calibration questions single splits vary, as the 90th-percentile column shows.

How it was measured, and the limitsread before trusting the numbers
  • 104 questions over 94 pages of the public GitLab Handbook: facts, paraphrases, numbers, cross-country comparisons, and 23 questions the handbook cannot answer. 12 unsound drafts were removed, with reasons published.
  • “Wrong answers” counts answers shown to the user that are wrong or invented; a partly right answer counts as half.
  • Assistant qwen/qwen3.8-27b; grader openai/gpt-oss-120b, a different model family. The grader was checked against blind grades from an independent LLM reviewer of a third family (agreement κ = 0.93, n = 42). That is a cross-check between models, not a human gold standard.
  • Only 14 comparison questions, so that number has a wide interval (25% → 64%).
  • One corpus, one assistant model. Your system will behave differently, which is the reason to measure it.
  • Every model response is committed, so all numbers replay offline without an API key.
